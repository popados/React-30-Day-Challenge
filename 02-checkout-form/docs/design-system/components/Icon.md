Line icons from Lucide (v1.48.0, ISC License): 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps, coloured by the surrounding text colour.

**Consumer provides:** `name`, optional `size` (20 inside controls and messages, 24 standalone) and `label` when the icon carries meaning without visible text.

- Colour with CSS `color` on the parent: `ink` by default, `graphite` for decoration inside fields (the picker's search icon), `moss` for success/info, `error` for errors.
- Icons sit beside words — never the only signal, never a lone tap target without a `label` and a 44px (`target-min`) hit area.
- Mapping on sign-up: `search` in the picker, `circle-alert` errors, `info` the domain shortcut, `circle-check` success, `landmark` institutions, `mail` the email path, `external-link` when a step leaves Fieldnote.
- In notebooks: `notebook-pen`, `book-open`, `file-text`, `bookmark`, `library`, `quote`, `highlighter`.
- Need one that isn't here? Take it from Lucide at the same version, unchanged; don't mix in another icon family.
