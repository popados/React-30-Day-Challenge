Official third-party sign-in logos, for the Google and Apple buttons only — Fieldnote has no logo of its own yet (set the name in Source Serif 4 instead).

- `google-g.svg` — the standard four-colour Google "G" at 20px, taken unchanged from Google's own Sign in with Google branding sample (developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines). Use at 20px; Google forbids resizing or recolouring it.
- `apple-left-black-medium.svg` — Apple's "Logo - SIWA - Left-aligned - Black - Medium": black logo on a white tile, 31 × 44, for white buttons. The one the Apple button uses.
- `apple-left-white-medium.svg` — Apple's white-logo-on-black version, for black buttons.
- `apple-logo-only-black.svg`, `apple-logo-only-white.svg` — Apple's logo-only square buttons (56 × 56), for icon-only sign-in.

The Apple files are unchanged from Apple's Logo-Sign-in-with-Apple.dmg (developer.apple.com/design/resources). Both marks are trademarks of their owners; follow Google's branding guidelines and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for Sign in with Apple.
