pocket@shell: type logo and icon sheet

The wordmark is JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, outlined, with the same @ as the app icon picked out in color and the letters a step quieter. Each row shows the icon (128, 64, 32, 16px), the wordmark on that icon's background, and the icon + wordmark lockup on white and on dark. Contrast pills: the wordmark is large text, so WCAG AA needs 3:1 on its banner; the @ in lockups is held to 4.5:1.
