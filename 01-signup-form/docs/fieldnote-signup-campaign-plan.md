# Campaign brief: "In one place"

Fieldnote sign-up campaign · 12 weeks · organic only · Nov 2, 2026 – Jan 24, 2027

Oct 11, 2026 · Companion to the brand voice guidelines in [../.claude/brand-voice-guidelines.md](../.claude/brand-voice-guidelines.md)

Assumptions: all copy follows the Fieldnote brand voice guidelines. The plan has no historical data behind it, so the targets below are starting points to adjust in week 4.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** In one place. It comes from the headline "Your research notes, in one place".
- **Summary:** Get researchers to create Fieldnote accounts with the login they already have at their university. The push builds through the end-of-term thesis crunch and peaks at the start of the spring term.
- **Primary objective:** 1,000 new accounts by Jan 24, 2027.
- **Secondary objectives:**
  - 40% of new accounts create a first notebook within 7 days.
  - 25 institutions with at least 10 sign-ups each, as evidence for future partnership talks.
  - 1,500 email subscribers, including people who aren't ready to sign up yet.

**Why this timing:** November and December are the end-of-term crunch for papers and theses, when note chaos hurts most. The holiday weeks are quiet, so they're used for preparation. Early January is when researchers set up tools for the new term, which is the strongest moment for sign-ups.

## 2. Target audience

| Segment | Role in campaign | Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Graduate students** (primary) | Volume of sign-ups | Master's or PhD student writing a thesis, with notes scattered across PDFs, docs and a reference manager. Wants one place where a highlight, its source and their thinking stay together. Finds tools through peers, Reddit, academic YouTube and Bluesky, and through library workshops. |
| **Faculty** (secondary) | Multiplier | Recommends tools to labs, seminars and advisees. Cares about citation accuracy and not adding admin work. Reachable through LinkedIn, Bluesky, department newsletters and colleagues. |
| **Research staff and librarians** (secondary) | Multiplier and channel | Runs research-skills workshops and maintains tool guides (LibGuides). Cares about privacy, institutional access and supporting students. Reachable through library listservs and professional communities. |

- **Pain points:** notes separated from their sources, lost highlights, re-finding a quote at 2 a.m., one more password to manage, losing access after graduating or changing institution.
- **Motivations:** finishing the thesis or paper, citing correctly, a calmer research workflow.
- **Buying stage:** mostly awareness and consideration. The product is new, and the institution sign-in removes most friction at the decision point.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Your research notes, in one place. Sign in with your university login.

| # | Supporting message | Answers | Proof point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Notes sit beside their sources, organised into a notebook for each project or thesis. | Scattered notes | A product demo showing one notebook: a highlight, its citation and a note |
| 2 | No new password. Sign in with your university login. | Account fatigue | The institution sign-in flow takes under 30 seconds (time it before claiming this) |
| 3 | Your library access carries over, and you can import from Zotero. | Switching cost | Show the Zotero import (needs confirmation, see Risks) |
| 4 | Free for students and researchers at participating institutions. | Cost | The institution count is **not yet confirmed**. Use "participating institutions" until it is. |

**Variations by channel:** the voice stays the same everywhere, with no exclamation marks, no emoji and sentence case.

- **Reddit and communities:** first person from the team, upfront about who we are, and help before mentioning the product.
- **Librarian outreach:** formal, privacy-first, focused on student support.
- **Bluesky and LinkedIn:** short research moments ("A highlight, its source and your note on it, on one page.").

## 4. Channel strategy

Everything here costs time rather than money. The effort levels assume a team of one or two people.

| Channel | Type | Why it fits | Formats | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sign-up page and website** | Owned | Every channel sends people here, so it's the conversion point | The sign-up screen, value bullets, sample notebook | Medium, once |
| **Email** | Owned | Captures people who aren't ready yet, and drives activation after sign-up | Waitlist and newsletter (every two weeks), a 4-email onboarding sequence | Low-Medium |
| **Blog and SEO** | Owned | Grad students search for workflow help, and the content keeps working after the campaign | 6 posts: thesis note systems, a Zotero plus notes workflow, a citation hygiene checklist | Medium |
| **Library and grad-school partnerships** | Earned | **Highest-leverage channel.** Librarians and graduate student associations reach students directly and are trusted. | A workshop kit, a one-page explainer for LibGuides, a short slide deck | High |
| **Bluesky and LinkedIn** | Owned | Where academics and faculty talk about tools | 3 posts a week: research moments, short demos, tips | Medium |
| **Reddit and academic communities** | Earned | Large grad-student audiences (r/GradSchool, r/PhD, discipline subs) | Helpful answers and an occasional transparent "we built this" post where the rules allow it | Medium |
| **Academic creators** | Earned | Academic YouTubers and newsletter writers shape students' tool choices | Free extended access plus an early look; no paid placements | Medium |
| **Referrals** | Owned | Labs and cohorts adopt tools together | An "Invite your lab" link after the first notebook is created | Low, if the product supports it |

**Left out on purpose:** paid ads (no budget), Product Hunt (wrong audience), press (wait until there's adoption data).

## 7. Success metrics

| KPI | Target | How it's tracked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New accounts (primary)** | 1,000 by Jan 24 | Sign-up events tagged by channel |
| Activation | 40% create a notebook within 7 days | Product event "first notebook created" |
| Sign-up method mix | 50% or more via institution sign-in | Sign-up method field |
| Institutions with 10 or more sign-ups | 25 | Email domain or institution at sign-up |
| Email subscribers | 1,500 | Email tool |
| Library or student-association placements | 10 workshops or tool-guide listings | A simple tracking sheet |

**Reporting cadence:** a weekly 15-minute check on sign-ups by channel and activation, a full review in week 4 to reallocate effort, and a retrospective in week 12. The analytics and tagged links need to be in place in week 1, or the results can't be attributed to channels.

## 8. Budget allocation

There's no spend, so the constraint is **time**, roughly 15–20 hours a week:

| Activity | Share of time |
| --- | --- |
| Library and partnership outreach | 30% |
| Content (blog, demos, email) | 30% |
| Social and community | 25% |
| Measurement and iteration | 10% |
| Contingency and reactive work | 5% |

If a small budget becomes available later, the best first spend is **design help on the workshop kit**, then **boosting the January posts that performed best**, before any search ads.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **The sign-up flow isn't ready.** Today the app has the components but no sign-up screen. | Treat week 1 as a hard gate. If it slips, collect waitlist sign-ups and move the soft launch, but keep the January peak fixed. |
| **Unverified claims.** "1,200+ institutions", "library access carries over" and the Zotero import are all unconfirmed, and the docs use a real university in examples. | Use "participating institutions" until the number is confirmed, demo only features that ship, and use fictional institutions in every published asset. |
| **Few institutions are connected**, so most visitors can't use the main path. | The email sign-up path still works. The "not on Fieldnote yet, we'll link it when they join" copy turns this into a reason to contact the library. Track requests to prioritise partnerships. |
| **Community self-promotion bans** | Read each community's rules first, lead with help, post as an identified team member, and limit posting frequency. |
| **Holiday lull** | Treat it as prep time. Don't judge performance on weeks 8–9. |
